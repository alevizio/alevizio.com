Alejandro Vizio

Product & Brand Designer — San Francisco

Alejandro Vizio is a product and brand designer based in San Francisco who designs product and brand as one system — so a company's interface and its identity feel like a single thing.

Most designers do product or brand. Alejandro does both, and treats them as one: the interface, the UX, the design system, and the visual identity, shaped together from early concepts to polished systems used by real people.

He was a partner and later CEO at Aerolab (8+ years) and led design at Pachama (5+ years) — a forest-carbon company acquired by Carbon Direct in 2025. Climate and sustainability run through his work via Pachama and Earthscale.

Beyond that, he has worked on product and brand for Planta, Messa, Earthscale, Limai, Fudo, and Djooni. Outside design, he is also a DJ and music producer.
