Alejandro Vizio — Product & Brand Designer in San Francisco

Alejandro Vizio (alevizio) is a product & brand designer in San Francisco who designs product and brand as one system — so the interface and the identity feel like a single thing, from early concepts to polished systems used by real people.

He builds and shapes companies at founder and owner level: co-founder of Aerolab (8+ years) and design at Pachama (5+ years, acquired by Carbon Direct in 2025). He has led product and brand work for Planta, Messa, Earthscale, Limai, Fudo, and Djooni. Climate is a through-line via Pachama and Earthscale. Outside design he is also a DJ and music producer.

Based in San Francisco, CA, USA. Contact: viziomas@gmail.com.
