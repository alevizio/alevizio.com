Alejandro Vizio — Product & Brand Designer in San Francisco

Alejandro Vizio (alevizio) is a product & brand designer in San Francisco who designs product and brand as one system — so the interface and the identity feel like a single thing, from early concepts to polished systems used by real people.

He was a partner and later CEO at Aerolab (8+ years) and led design at Pachama (5+ years, acquired by Carbon Direct in 2025). He has led product and brand work for Planta, Messa, Earthscale, Limai, Fudo, and Djooni. Climate is a through-line via Pachama and Earthscale. Outside design he is also a DJ and music producer.

Born in Buenos Aires in 1985, he grew up there, in Madrid and in Lake Forest, Illinois, and studied graphic design at ORT. He joined Aerolab in 2012 as art director and third partner, moved to San Francisco as its US manager in 2017 and became its CEO in 2018, then joined Pachama in 2020. His timeline, 1985 to 2026.
